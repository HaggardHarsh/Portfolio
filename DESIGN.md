# The woodland cabin

An immersive portfolio for recruiters and employers, expressed as a short forest walk into one inhabited timber cabin. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. Normal stylized 3D is the default, with a full-world coloured ASCII toggle. Third-person shoulder view and first-person view share the same physical space.

The trail, warm porch lamp and cabin roof establish visual hierarchy. Dense trees frame the approach while preserving a sightline to the door. Locally bundled Quaternius nature and animated character assets give the scene layered silhouettes and natural movement. Material families are wood, stone, foliage, warm metal and cream paper. Shadow, haze and sunlight establish depth rather than decorating HTML panels.

One workbench, an open diary, a calling card and a tool board are the four information anchors. Solid cream reading panels and system sans-serif preserve reading comfort. Serif display text introduces the personal invitation, while compact map labels and controls remain conventional HTML. Rendering mode never transforms portfolio copy into unreadable glyphs.

All motion serves travel, camera following or environmental presence. Ambient motion can be paused; reduced motion disables it by default and stops character animation. Sound is opt-in. There is no automatic camera tour, combat, jumping, collectible gate, or invented personal history. Direct content links and the HTML reading view are always available.
