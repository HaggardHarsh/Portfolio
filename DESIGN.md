# The woodland cabin

An immersive portfolio for recruiters and employers, expressed as a short forest walk into one inhabited timber cabin. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. Normal stylized 3D is the default, with a full-world coloured ASCII toggle. Third-person shoulder view and first-person view share the same physical space.

The trail, warm porch lamp and cabin roof establish visual hierarchy. Dense trees frame the approach while preserving a sightline to the door. Locally bundled Quaternius nature and animated character assets give the scene layered silhouettes and natural movement. Material families are wood, stone, foliage, warm metal and cream paper. Shadow, haze and sunlight establish depth rather than decorating HTML panels.

One workbench, an open diary, a calling card and a tool board are the four information anchors. A forest-green archive spine holds the chapter navigation; warm paper pages carry the content. Serif titles and diary prose give the pages a bookish character, while system sans-serif supports project descriptions, evidence and controls. Rendering mode never transforms portfolio copy into unreadable glyphs.

All motion serves travel, camera following or environmental presence. Ambient motion can be paused; reduced motion disables it by default and stops character animation. Sound is opt-in. There is no automatic camera tour, combat, jumping, collectible gate, or invented personal history. Direct content links and the HTML reading view are always available.

Edition 07 adds a more grounded material and character treatment. A textured male human replaces the neutral jointed mannequin; native breathing/idle and retargeted walk/run clips preserve recognizable anatomy. The canvas backpack follows the upper spine. Scanned weathered boards, slate, mossy stone, leaf litter and worn soil establish material scale and age. Normal and roughness maps add surface response without disturbing navigation geometry. Softer neutral daylight preserves texture detail while cabin lamps remain warm. Curved grass blades and tree-model impostors replace visibly geometric vegetation placeholders.

Edition 08 turns the reading dialogs into a cabin archive. The diary uses a personal opening, a marginal note and a typeset first-name sign-off. The workbench mounts real project screenshots with captions, build notes and repository links. Skills form a ruled inventory with project evidence beside each tool. Contact is an inset calling card with direct email and profile links. The index connects these compositions without duplicating their contents.

The thin brass rule repeats the cabin's warm metal accent. A botanical sprig on the spine connects the archive to its woodland setting; the quiet five-pixel paper grain suggests a physical page without competing with text. Inset spine shading indicates a binding, image shadows indicate mounted prints, and the dialog shadow separates the reading surface from the forest. Directional arrows identify chapter travel or outbound destinations. This surface uses ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1; the surrounding world retains its existing dials. Chapters change immediately to preserve contrast, and the world remains paused while reading.

On phones the spine becomes a compact header and a horizontally scrollable chapter strip. The page scrolls independently beneath a sticky close control. The ledger and profile links stack into one column. Native dialogs, keyboard focus containment, Escape, focus return and direct fragment links remain part of the reading experience.
